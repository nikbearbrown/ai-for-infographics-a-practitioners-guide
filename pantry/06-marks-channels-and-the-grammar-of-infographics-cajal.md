# CAJAL — Chapter 6: Marks, Channels, and the Grammar of Infographics

## Figure 6.1 — The Cleveland–McGill accuracy ladder
- **Concept:** Visual channels ranked from most to least accurate for decoding quantity.
- **Type:** Ranked ladder / ordered list diagram.
- **Shows:** Seven elementary perceptual tasks (position → length → angle → area → volume → hue) with position at the top as the channel the eye reads best.
- **Scope:** One axis of decreasing accuracy; one accent.
- **Red accent:** "Position along a common scale" — the top rung, the one channel a relationship-first design should climb toward.

## Figure 6.2 — Relationship-first: the energy mix corrected
- **Concept:** The chart type is an *output* of the relationship-and-task decision, never an input.
- **Type:** Two-column before/after decision diagram.
- **Shows:** Form-first choices (pie, bubble) on weak channels vs. relationship-first choices (ordered bars) on position.
- **Scope:** Single relationship (ranking) held fixed; weak vs. corrected encoding.
- **Red accent:** The corrected bar that climbs to the position channel (the single emphasized "right answer").
