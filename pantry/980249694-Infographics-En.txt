What is an infographic?
  Infographics are graphic visual representations of
  information, data or knowledge intended to
  present information quickly and clearly.
  Wikipedia

                               An infographic is a visual representation of information
                               in the form of a chart, graph, or other image accompanied
                               by minimal text, intended to give an easily understood
                               overview, often of a complex subject.
                               dictionary.com



              An infographic tells a story with images and text. The
              images are not only illustrative but also information carriers.

A checklist for the perfect data visual
  1. Is my message clear?
  2. Do I really need a chart?
  3. Did I choose the best chart type?
  4. Is my chart easy to understand
     without additional explanation?
  5. Can I remove superfluous ‘ink’
     to increase the data-to-ink ratio?
  6. Are my colours clear and accessible?
  7. Are my charts consistent
     throughout my article, poster, or thesis?
